# ALL OR SOMETHING — CLEAR THE AIR
## Refined short • United Road Original

Target: approximately 85–90 seconds, portrait 9:16. Timing is an editorial guide, not measured voice duration. Fictional football satire; dialogue is invented.

Carrick and Bruno finally have an honest conversation. Behind them, Jason Wilcox tries to turn a fragile reconciliation into a successful corporate announcement. Just as the two find common ground, Gary Neville arrives to inspect the standards.

### 00:00–00:07 | A private conversation
Carrington. Manager's office. Carrick sits left, Bruno right. Behind them, Wilcox stands beside a flip chart: CLEAR THE AIR. Beneath it, in smaller writing: NO LEAKS.

NARRATOR [grave, understated]: “Carrington. A private meeting to clear the air. Jason had invited a camera crew.”

Wilcox subtly waves the camera closer. Carrick notices. Bruno hasn't looked up.

### 00:07–00:24 | The problem
CARRICK [quiet, firm]: “Bruno, if you've got a problem with the tactics, tell me. Not the television.”

BRUNO [earnest, defensive]: “I tried. You said your door was always open.”

CARRICK [plainly]: “It is.”

BRUNO [small glance towards Jason]: “Jason was standing in it.”

Cut to Wilcox, still blocking the doorway. Without speaking, he shuffles six inches sideways. He remains mostly in the doorway.

### 00:24–00:41 | An honest exchange
CARRICK [trying to be reasonable]: “Look. Maybe I could've been clearer.”

BRUNO [softens]: “And maybe I shouldn't have said it on telly.”

CARRICK [tentative]: “So we're all right?”

BRUNO [completely sincere]: “Yes. I still think the tactics were shit.”

CARRICK [dry, no anger]: “Aye. But now you've told me indoors. Progress.”

A tiny, reluctant smile from Bruno. Hold long enough to show that Carrick has accepted the criticism without surrendering the room.

### 00:41–00:56 | Clearing the air
Behind them, Wilcox ticks RESOLVED before either man has moved. He produces a blue aerosol and sprays twice into the empty space above his own shoulder.

BRUNO [turning, baffled]: “Jason. What are you doing?”

WILCOX [matter-of-fact]: “Clearing the air. It's on the agenda.”

CARRICK [looks at the can, then Jason]: “That's air freshener.”

WILCOX [quiet corporate pride]: “Fresh start.”

Carrick and Bruno exchange their first genuinely shared look: the same exhausted disbelief.

### 00:56–01:07 | The reconciliation
CARRICK [low, to Bruno]: “Next time, just come straight to me.”

BRUNO [a little smile]: “Move Jason first.”

They shake hands. Behind them, Wilcox turns over his prepared announcement: UNITED BEHIND THE MANAGER. He raises it for the camera. Neither man notices.

### 01:07–01:30 | Standards inspection
The door opens sharply. Gary Neville stands there in a suit, blue lanyard and clipboard. He has appointed himself an inspector.

GARY [brisk authority]: “Surprise standards inspection.”

WILCOX [irritated, smile still fixed]: “Gary, you're not Ofsted.”

GARY [ignoring him]: “Who's in charge here?”

Carrick and Bruno immediately point at Wilcox. Together. Hold the tableau for one full second. Wilcox lowers his announcement.

WILCOX [smaller voice]: “It's a collaborative structure.”

Gary writes without looking up.

GARY [flat verdict]: “Special measures.”

Cut to Carrick and Bruno, now sitting side by side in shared resignation. Behind them, Wilcox lowers the can below the table. One tiny accidental PSSHT. Hard cut to black.
