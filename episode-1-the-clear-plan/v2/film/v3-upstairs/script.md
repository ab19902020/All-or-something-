# ALL OR SOMETHING — Episode One: THE CLEAR PLAN — UPSTAIRS (after Atlético, before The Mole)

**Animated parody.** Real events: after Atlético Madrid 2–0 United (Champions League, 13 October 2026) Lisandro
Martínez told Amazon Prime the players expected more "from upstairs" and from the technical director; he repeated his
frustration in a second interview; he then met Jason Wilcox and Michael Carrick at Carrington, a statement followed
(reports differ on whether it was an apology) and he was reported to have been fined (Sky Sports, ESPN, Goal,
14–22 October 2026). His real words are given by the narrator as paraphrase over a card that names its sources; the
lines after it carry the PARODY DIALOGUE label. The board meeting is invented.
