# ALL OR SOMETHING — Episode One: THE CLEAR PLAN (version 2)

**An animated parody.** Real results; the private scenes and dialogue are fictionalised. Version 2 remakes the whole
episode in one pipeline: every character is the same drawing and the same cloned Fish Audio voice from the first
frame to the last, the narrator runs through the whole episode, and the edit is tight (results land under narration,
no silent establishing shots, no dead holds).

Line IDs: `[M..]` the meeting, `[H..]` Hull, `[R..]` the month, `[L...]` The Mole (its original numbering),
`[C..]` Leeds to the end. Stage directions in italics.

---
